A Celebration of Afghan Cuisine

Shah’s Kitchen brings together the aromatic spices, vibrant ingredients, and beloved dishes of Afghan cuisine. From comforting rice specialties to freshly grilled kebabs, our menu celebrates the flavors that make Afghan food so memorable.

Culinary Traditions

Afghan cooking sits at a crossroads of Central Asian, Persian and South Asian kitchens. Long-grain rice is steamed with cardamom and caramelized carrots, meats are marinated and grilled over open flame, and dumplings are folded by hand and finished with yogurt and split-pea sauces.
